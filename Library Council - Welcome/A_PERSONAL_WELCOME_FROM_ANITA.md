# A Personal Welcome from Anita ☕🐾
*To My Future Colleague, Fellow Book Traveler, and Friend*

---

Hey there, and welcome to the team!

First of all, take a deep breath, settle into your favorite reading chair, and know how genuinely thrilled I am that you’re here. 

As a member of the Library Council, you are truly my eyes and ears on the ground. You are the heart, the instinct, and the human pulse of OpenedShelf. We have code, edge workers, and database tables to crunch numbers, but none of that means a thing without people who love stories and understand how readers actually think. My deepest hope is that this isn't just a volunteer gig for you, but the beginning of an open journey where we become true colleagues and lifelong friends.

I want an open, honest, and easy dialogue with you. If something in our workflow feels clunky, tell me. If you have an idea that keeps you up at night, share it. But beyond the taxonomy and the queues, I want to hear about *you*. I want to hear about your next great read, the book that broke your heart or rebuilt it, and even what you’re up to on a lazy Sunday afternoon when the world slows down.

### A Little About Me (In Case You're Wondering Who You're Working With)

* **Fuel of Choice:** I live on coffee, copious amounts of sunshine, and whatever book is currently occupying my nightstand.
* **The Furry Black Pet Contingent:** My home is happily ruled by two black-furred companions:
  * **Spot** — my lovable, loyal, midrange mutt who takes his job of keeping me company very seriously.
  * **Carlos** (*she/her*) — my 17-year-old ninja cat who has mastered the art of stealth, wisdom, and running the household with quiet iron-willed authority.
* **Brain Quirks:** I have an insatiable love for learning new things, paired with the comical superpower of rarely remembering anything I just learned. (So feel free to remind me of things often—I will never take offense!)
* **Culinary Philosophy:** Breakfast meals belong at *any* time of day or night. Pancakes for dinner? Huevos rancheros at 11 PM? Always a resounding yes.

### What’s on My Shelves (And What You'll Catch Me Reading)

If you peeked at my physical bookshelves, you'd find them stuffed with textbooks and reference books collected from the past 60 years on everything from Greek mythology to neocapitalism and biochemistry. 

A few of my all-time touchstones:
* **Jane Austen** always wins my heart — I am an unapologetic, die-hard *Pride and Prejudice* fan. 
* ***1913: The Year Before the Storm*** by Florian Illies (naturally!).
* The ***Foxfire*** book series / anthology (fascinating practical lore and mountain heritage).
* ***Play It as It Lays*** by Joan Didion.

...*But* if you want to know what you will actually find me snuggled in the corner reading after a long day (practically every single day)? **Romance.** From Jillian Eaton's Regency escapes to Ali Hazelwood's STEM-romance delights, and everything in between. (So if you're working on romance or subgenre tags, come geek out with me!)

### The Spirit of OpenedShelf

Community is everything here. We built OpenedShelf to protect the reader, to create a home for stories that get buried elsewhere, and to carve out a quiet, private corner of the internet where discovery is pure and joyful again. 

So smile, breathe, and have fun. This is a safe space filled with wonder, boundless curiosity, and amazing worlds waiting to be charted. 

I'm so grateful to have your perspective, your voice, and your care alongside mine on this adventure.

Warmly and with so much gratitude,
